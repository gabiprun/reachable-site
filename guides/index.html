Guides

Plain-language guides to the money, discounts and services you may already qualify for in Victoria, BC. No jargon, no sign-up, no cost.

Every number in these guides comes from a government or organisation page we read on the date shown, and every guide lists its sources at the bottom. Amounts change — usually each January — so if a guide looks old, phone the number on it and check.

Not sure where to start?

Two good ways in:

  • Read Start here: how the benefits system fits together — it maps the whole thing on one page and tells you which door to knock on first.
  • Or answer a few questions and let us suggest programs: use the benefits finder.

You can also just phone us and we will read any of this out to you, at your pace.

How these guides are written

We write these the way we would explain something to a friend at a kitchen table.

  • Every figure is traced to a source. If we could not verify a number, we say "check the current amount" instead of guessing.
  • We say when a programme does not exist or has ended. Sending you chasing something that is gone wastes your time and your bus fare.
  • We tell you what to say. Most guides include the words to use on the phone, because that is usually the hard part.
  • We say what to do when the answer is no. A lot of first answers are wrong, and a lot of denials get overturned.

Found something out of date or wrong? Please tell us — email hello@reachablesociety.ca or phone the Line. Corrections from people who have actually been through the process are the most valuable thing we get.

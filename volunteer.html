Volunteer with Reachable

A few hours a month can put help within someone's reach.

Tell us you are interested

Ways to help

Benefits navigator

Sit with a person, by phone or in person, and help them understand a benefit and fill in the application. Help them get the doctor's part signed. You need patience and a calm voice. We train you on the programs. You never give medical, legal, or financial advice; you help with forms and phone calls. A criminal record check with vulnerable-sector screening is required.

Callback volunteer

The Reachable Line takes callback requests from people who need a person. You call them back, look things up, and help them find the right office. You need a phone, a quiet place, and a few hours a week or a month. A criminal record check with vulnerable-sector screening is required.

Tech volunteer

Build and hand off websites, donation pages, sign-up forms, and small automations for tiny nonprofits, using simple tools, then teach the group's owner to run it. Good for people who like finishing things and writing clear instructions. Comfort with website builders, spreadsheets, and forms is enough; you do not need to be a programmer.

Board director

Help govern the society. See About.

What to expect

  1. Fill in the form below, or call us.
  2. We have a conversation about what you would like to do and how much time you have.
  3. For roles that talk with the people we serve, we ask for two references and a criminal record check with vulnerable-sector screening. We help you arrange it.
  4. Training, at your pace. Then you start alongside an experienced volunteer.

Our promises to you

  • Volunteering never costs you money.
  • We keep your information private, and we expect the same about the people you help.
  • You can change roles, take a break, or stop at any time.
  • We adapt roles to what you can do. Volunteers with disabilities are welcome and wanted.

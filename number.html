Reachable Number

A free phone number that is yours to keep, even when your phone changes.

Ask for a Reachable Number How to check your messages

Who it's for

Anyone who does not have one steady phone number. For example, you might be:

  • staying in a shelter, or moving between places;
  • using a prepaid phone that runs out, or a number that changes;
  • often losing a phone, or having it stolen;
  • borrowing other people's phones;
  • waiting on a doctor, a housing worker, an employer, or family, and afraid of missing the call.

What you get

  • A permanent local phone number, free. Put it on forms. Give it to your doctor, your housing worker, an employer, your family. It stays the same for years.
  • Calls follow you. Calls to your number ring whichever phone you have this week. Change which phone that is any time, from any phone, with your PIN. This is called "follow-me forwarding".
  • Voicemail that never fills a phone. If nobody answers, or if you have no phone right now, callers leave a message.
  • Hear your messages from any phone. Call the access line from any phone at all, enter your number and your PIN, and listen.
  • A web page for your messages. Log in with your number and PIN at a library or on a borrowed phone to hear your messages and change forwarding. (The web page opens when the service launches.)
  • Optional alerts. If you want, we send a text or an email when a new message arrives.

How to start

  1. Ask for a number: use the form below, call us, or ask a worker at your shelter or agency to send it for you.
  2. We set up your number and a PIN, and give them to you by phone, in person, or through your worker. That is all we need. No ID, no address, no email required.
  3. Tell us which phone to ring this week, or none. You can change it whenever you like.
  4. Give out your new number.

How to use it from any phone

Call the Reachable Number access line (the number is at the bottom of this page). Then:

  • Press 1 to hear your messages. Enter your Reachable Number and your PIN.
  • Press 2 to change which phone we ring. Enter your number and PIN, then the 10-digit phone number to ring. Enter nothing to send everything to voicemail.
  • Press 3 for the Reachable Line, the free help line.
  • Press 0 to hear the choices again.

Shortcut: call your own Reachable Number and press the star key (*) while the greeting plays. Enter your PIN to get straight to your messages.

Lost your PIN? Call us or ask your worker to. We will check who you are and set a new one.

Keeping your number

Your number is yours as long as you use it. If a number has not been used for about 90 days, we try to reach you first, through every contact you gave us. We never reuse a number without giving notice.

What we will never do

  • Charge you. Not now, not later.
  • Listen to your messages. Only you, with your PIN. We open a mailbox only if you ask us to fix a problem with it, and we tell you when we do.
  • Give your number, your messages, or your callers to anyone else, unless the law requires it.
  • Require ID, an address, an email, or a phone of your own.
  • Take your number away without trying to reach you first.

Ask for a Reachable Number

We only need a name and one way to reach you to set things up. Everything else is optional.

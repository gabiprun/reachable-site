Reachable Benefits

Find out which benefits you may already qualify for. Then get a person to help you apply.

Start the benefits finder Help me apply

Who it's for

  • People with a disability or a long-term health problem.
  • Seniors, especially on a low income.
  • Family members and caregivers helping someone else.
  • Anyone who suspects there is money or help they are not getting.

Many benefits are only paid to people who know to ask, and the forms can be hard. That is what we help with.

What you get

  • The benefits finder on this page. A few questions, no personal details, and a list of programs you may qualify for, each with the phone number to call.
  • Plain-language information about each program: what it is, who qualifies, how to apply, and where the information came from.
  • A person to help you apply. A trained Reachable volunteer, called a navigator, sits with you by phone or in person, helps you fill in the forms, and helps with the part a doctor has to sign.
  • The same help by phone. Call the Reachable Line and ask about any benefit. You do not need this website.

How to start

  1. Answer the questions below. Skip anything you are not sure about.
  2. Read your results. Each one has a "Call" button for the office that runs the program.
  3. Press "Help me apply" and a volunteer will get in touch.

What we will never do

  • Charge you, or take a share of your benefit. Some companies charge a cut of your back-pay to fill in the Disability Tax Credit form. We never will.
  • Ask for your Social Insurance Number, bank details, or passwords on this website or by phone. When a navigator helps you with a real form, you keep your papers, and we do not keep copies.
  • Give medical, legal, or financial advice. We explain programs and help with forms. Doctors, lawyers, and advocates do the rest, and we can tell you who to call.
  • Apply for anything without you. You see and sign everything.
